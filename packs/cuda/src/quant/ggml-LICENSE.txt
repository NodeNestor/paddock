The i-quant codebooks in iq_grids.cuh and in
crates/paddock-kernels/src/reference/iq_grids.rs (the IQ1_S, IQ2_XXS,
IQ2_XS, IQ2_S, IQ3_XXS, IQ3_S grids and the IQ4_NL value table) are the
format definition of ggml's i-quant types, transcribed from ggml-common.h
in ggml / llama.cpp. They are used under ggml's licence, reproduced below.
No other ggml code is used.
